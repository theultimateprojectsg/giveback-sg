A Question Worth Asking

Where does your money
actually go?

Giving Tree never touches, holds, or sees your money. Here's exactly what happens when you donate.

📱
You scan PayNow
Your bank app opens. You confirm the amount, directly through PayNow's own secure system.
→
🏛️
Charity's own bank account
Funds land directly in the charity's UEN-linked account. Giving Tree never receives, holds, or routes your money.
→
🧾
Your receipt is recorded
We simply record that the donation happened, so your receipt and tax deduction are ready when you need them.
🔒 Giving Tree is not a payment processor. We never see your bank details.
