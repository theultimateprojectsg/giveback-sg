Who We Built This For

For the small teams carrying big hearts.

Behind every IPC-registered charity in Singapore is a small team doing extraordinary work. Most don't have a finance team, a tech team, or even a full-time admin. Just people who care deeply — and not enough hours in the day.

"I'm a volunteer. Doing this admin was never part of the job."

"Our cash donations are in an envelope. I don't know how to issue receipts for 200 donors."

"We do incredible work in our community. Nobody outside our circle knows we exist."

Giving Tree was built for them. And it's completely free.

Receipts & IRAS in one click
Download a fully formatted IRAS file for myTax Portal in seconds. Never miss the 31 January deadline again.
Thank every donor automatically
Personalised thank you emails sent automatically when you confirm a donation. Every donor acknowledged — no one forgotten.
Missing NRICs flagged for you
Donors without NRICs highlighted automatically. One-click reminder emails so they can claim their 250% tax deduction.
Donor analytics that show your impact
Monthly trends, top donors, donation size breakdown — simple honest analytics that show the impact you're making.
Every donation captured, however it arrives
Flag day cash, charity dinners, bank transfers — enter manually and they flow into the same dashboard and IRAS export.
Full donor management
Every donor in one place. Giving history, receipt status, NRIC, contact details. No more spreadsheets scattered across inboxes.
