Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

1. Who We Are

Giving Tree ("we", "us", "our") operates givingtree.sg and charity.givingtree.sg, a platform that connects individual donors with Institution of a Public Character (IPC) and other registered charities in Singapore. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is protected, in accordance with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

2. What We Collect

From donors, when you create an account:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • NRIC or FIN (optional at signup, required before claiming a 250% tax deduction on a donation to an IPC charity)
  • Donation history (charity, amount, date) recorded when you give through the platform

From charities, when a charity account is created:

  • Charity name and UEN (Unique Entity Number) — already public information
  • Staff login email address
  • Donor records the charity enters manually (e.g. cash, cheque, or bank transfer donations), which may include a donor's name, email, and NRIC if provided

3. Why We Collect It

  • NRIC: collected solely to generate the IRAS-required record for the 250% tax deduction on donations to IPC charities. We do not collect NRIC for any other purpose.
  • Name and email: to create and manage your account, issue receipts, and send donation confirmations.
  • Donation history: so you can view your own giving record and download tax-deduction summaries.
  • Charity UEN: to verify charity registration and route PayNow donations correctly.

4. How Your Money Moves

Giving Tree is not a payment processor. When you donate via PayNow, funds go directly from your bank to the charity's own bank account, linked to their UEN. We never receive, hold, or have access to donated funds at any point.

5. How Your Data Is Stored and Protected

  • Data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider, which encrypts data at rest at the infrastructure level.
  • NRIC is stored in a dedicated, access-restricted record, separate from general account information, and is only accessible to the donor themselves through their own account.
  • Wherever your NRIC is displayed within the app or on receipts, it is shown partially masked (e.g. S××××​×7A) and never in full.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Third Parties We Use

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (e.g. donation confirmations, thank-you emails)
  • Vercel — website hosting

These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own data protection terms. We do not control, and are not responsible for, their independent data practices.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data, including donation records and NRIC, for as long as your account remains active. If you wish to close your account and have your data deleted, you may request this at any time by contacting hello@givingtree.sg. We will delete your personal data within a reasonable period of receiving your request, except where retention is required to comply with a legal obligation.

8. Your Rights

Under the PDPA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent for the collection, use, or disclosure of your data, subject to legal or contractual restrictions

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@givingtree.sg.

9. Charity Access to Donor Data

When you donate to a specific charity, that charity can see your name, donation amount, date, and (where provided) NRIC for the purpose of issuing receipts and tax-deduction records. Charities cannot see your donation history with other charities.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via email or an in-app notice.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your personal data can be sent to hello@givingtree.sg.
