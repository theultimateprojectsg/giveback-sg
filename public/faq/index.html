Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions donors ask us most.

Trust & Safety
Does Giving Tree ever hold or touch my money?
No. Every donation goes through PayNow directly from your bank to the charity's own bank account, linked to their UEN. Giving Tree is never a payment intermediary — we don't receive, hold, or route any funds at any point.
Why do you need my NRIC?
IRAS requires it to issue the 250% tax deduction on your behalf — the same information any charity would need from you to claim it manually. It's asked for once at signup, stored in a separate access-restricted record, and shown masked everywhere in the app.
How do I know a charity on Giving Tree is legitimate?
Every charity listed has been checked against Singapore's official Charity Portal (charities.gov.sg) before being added. We follow the same "Ask, Check, Give" principle the Commissioner of Charities recommends to all donors.
How is this different from giving.sg?
giving.sg holds your donation and disburses it to charities on a schedule, currently charging a 1.8% + GST platform fee to do so. Giving Tree never holds your funds at all — PayNow sends your donation straight to the charity's own account, instantly, with no fee.
Donations & Tax
Does it cost anything to donate?
No. Giving Tree is completely free for donors — no fees, no commission. 100% of what you give reaches the charity.
Is there a minimum donation amount?
No minimum — give whatever amount feels right to you.
Which banks support PayNow?
All major Singapore banks work with PayNow, including DBS, POSB, OCBC, UOB, Standard Chartered, Citibank, HSBC, Maybank, and Bank of China. If your banking app has a QR code scanner, you're good to go.
I scanned the QR code but didn't tap "I've Completed Payment" in the app — is my donation recorded?
No. Scanning the QR and completing the bank transfer is the first step — you still need to tap "I've Completed Payment" in the Giving Tree app to log the donation and trigger your receipt. If you paid but forgot to tap, open the app, go to the same charity, enter the same amount, and confirm it there.
Do I need to declare my donation on my tax return myself?
No. Once the charity submits your donation to IRAS, your 250% deduction is automatically reflected in your tax assessment — there's nothing for you to manually declare.
What if a charity never issues my receipt?
Most receipts are issued once a charity confirms your donation. If one is taking longer than expected, write to us at hello@givingtree.sg and we'll follow up on your behalf.
Your Account
Do I need an account to donate?
Yes — an account is required so your donation history, receipts, and tax records are all in one place. Signing up takes under a minute and your NRIC is optional at that stage.
I made a donation by mistake — can I undo it?
Yes. From your Receipts screen, you can cancel any donation as long as the charity hasn't yet confirmed it. Once a charity has confirmed your payment, contact hello@givingtree.sg and we'll help sort it out directly with the charity.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes, at any time. Email hello@givingtree.sg and we'll delete your personal data within a reasonable period, as outlined in our Privacy Policy.

Still have a question? Write to us at hello@givingtree.sg — we reply to every message.
